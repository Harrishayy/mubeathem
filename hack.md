​What you'll build
​Mubit sits between your application and its model calls. It generates lightweight model updates from your calls and feedback, so the calls you already make become task-adapted open models. The base models stay fixed, and updates are built for fast refresh as your workload changes.

​The brief: build a product that routes its LLM calls through Mubit and turns your corrections into model updates for a recurring task.

​Pick a track:

​Recurring tasks. Choose a task your product runs again and again: extraction, triage, support replies, review, routing. Show your corrections becoming model updates.

​Own your intelligence layer. Take a workload you'd normally send to a closed model and move it onto a task-adapted open model. Show quality, cost and latency side by side.

​Fast refresh. Build for a workload that keeps changing: new data, new customers, new domains. Show your model updates keeping pace.

​The top 3 prizes are judged across all tracks.

​Four criteria, weighted equally:

​Use of Mubit. Show a correction turning into a model update in your demo.

​Usefulness. A real recurring task someone would want handled.

​Technical execution. It works, and it holds up under questions.
