Cue design system

A calm co-pilot for cold calls: one glance, one line, back to the conversation. One component set in three directions, built on Mubit's honey and sage. Rendered here from the real components and the real token CSS.

principlesdirectionstypetokensmomentssproutoverbarcomponentsprimitivesmotionbuild

Principles

  1. One thing at a time. Never more than one suggestion on screen. A new card cross-fades in over 150 ms with a 6 px lift.
  2. Readable at a glance. The line is set large (21 px Honey, 24 px Signal) in the voice face, Newsreader: what you say looks different from what the UI says.
  3. Colour means the moment. Eight families (gatekeeper slate, opener blue, cold ice, objection violet, signal green, close gold, compliance amber, neutral). The colour glows under Sprout in the bar before you read a word.
  4. Evidence, always. Every card says why it's there; fallbacks carry a quiet “model offline” note, never presented as the model; debug shows the path, latency and request id.
  5. Quiet when it should be. No card is a valid state with its own design, not an error.
  6. Respectful motion. Nothing bounces. Compliance pulses once; it is the only pulse. Reduced motion keeps fades and drops movement.
  7. Tokens only. Components never name a colour; a test fails if they do. Every text pair is contrast-checked in every direction.
  8. Every string, every size. 200-character companies, 320-character lines, unbroken tokens, emoji and CJK; 320 px to 2560 px. Playwright proves it.

Three directions, one component set

Honey (default): cream ground, honey accent, sage ink. Calm, warm, premium; light and dark. Night Shift: the Cluely-style overlay; near-black glass with a 1 px inner highlight and a honey glow; always dark. Signal: phones in hand; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, 24 px line, stronger moment colours, no translucency, AAA line text.

Newsreader is the voice face (Honey, Night): a text serif with optical sizes, so the line reads like something said rather than chrome. Schibsted Grotesk carries the UI: a sturdy newspaper grotesk with tabular figures for the timer. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next runs all of Signal, built for low vision. Spline Sans Mono for ids. Each has a metric-matched local fallback (size-adjust, ascent/descent overrides) so layout holds before fonts load.

Tokens

Semantic names (Casework method). Shared scales live in base.css: space on a 4 px base, type 11–34 px with the line at 21 px, radii by role, durations 120 / 180 / 240 / 360 ms, z-index, breakpoints 360 / 560 / 860 / 1120, hit targets 36 desktop / 44 touch, a 2 px focus ring. Direction files set colour, families and shape only. Tokens hang off [data-cue], never :root, so they work in a Shadow DOM.

Sprout

A seedling in a honey soil pod: the model growing on your team's calls. Inline SVG under 4 KB, pure function of mascotFor(view, connection) from core. Readable at 20 px.

The overbar at every size

Collapsed ≤ 56 px: Sprout · live timer · moment · the line (one line, fading) · receptivity · expand. The moment uses a whole-word label, never an ellipsis; container queries swap it for its icon (with a tooltip), then drop receptivity and the live word as the pill narrows. An accessory rides under the bar (above it when docked on a phone). On phones the panel opens as a half-height sheet. Expanded: the §4.1 panel; two columns from 860 px.
